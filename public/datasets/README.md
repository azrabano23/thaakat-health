# Research-image provenance

These assets are research examples shown separately from the synthetic Thaakat patient chart.
They must never be described as the demo patient's images or used as clinical evidence.

- `ut-endomri-example.png` and `ut-endomri-label.png` are the tutorial image and label supplied by
  the [RAovSeg repository](https://github.com/xlianguth/RAovSeg), whose README identifies
  UT-EndoMRI as the source. UT-EndoMRI is released on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0:
  https://zenodo.org/records/15750762
- `glenda-c135-frame.jpg` and `glenda-c135-mask.png` are frame `c_135_v_(video_4079.mp4)_f_229`
  and its released annotation from GLENDA v1.5:
  https://ftp.itec.aau.at/datasets/GLENDA/
  GLENDA is research-only / CC BY-NC 4.0. Retain attribution to Leibetseder et al. (2020) and
  confirm the intended use remains non-commercial before deploying outside the research demo.
