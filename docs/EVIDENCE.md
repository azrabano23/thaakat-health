## 1. Burden & delay (the on-page stats)

- **Prevalence — "1 in 10 women, ~190 million worldwide."** ✅ WHO, verbatim: *"Endometriosis affects an estimated 10% (190 million) of reproductive age women worldwide."* https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/endometriosis · US ~6.5M (commonly cited, not census).
- **Diagnostic delay.** ✅ WHO: *"between 4 and 12 years."* Nnoaham 2011 (WERF, 10 countries): **mean 6.7 yr**, longer in state-funded systems (8.3) than private (5.5). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21718982/ · Hadfield 1996: **US 11.7 yr**, UK 8.0 yr. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8671344/ · **Say "7–10 years and getting worse."** The round "7 doctors" is advocacy-sourced (attribute, don't assert). UK APPG (10,000+ patients): **58% saw their GP 10+ times** before diagnosis. https://www.endometriosis-uk.org/
- **Economic burden.** ✅ Simoens 2012 (Hum Reprod, WERF EndoCost, 909 women, 10 countries): **€9,579/woman/yr, of which productivity loss (€6,298) was 2× healthcare cost (€3,113)** — *most of the cost is lost life, not treatment.* https://academic.oup.com/humrep/article/27/5/1292/700268 · US: Soliman 2018 (Adv Ther, MarketScan): **~$16,573/patient** in the first year after dx. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12325-018-0667-3 · US aggregate ~$78–119B/yr is **modeled** — hedge; DIY-defensible = 6.5M × $10–16k ≈ $65–100B.
- **Diagnostic error at large.** ✅ Newman-Toker 2023 (BMJ Qual Saf, Johns Hopkins): **~795,000 Americans die or are permanently disabled by diagnostic error each year.** https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/news/newsroom/news-releases/2023/07/report-highlights-public-health-impact-of-serious-harms-from-diagnostic-error-in-us
- **The moat stat — "it was already in the scan."** ✅ Radiology retrospective error rate **~30%**, and **60–80% of radiology errors are perceptual** — the finding was *visible in retrospect* but missed on first read. Brady 2017, *Insights into Imaging*. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13244-016-0534-1
- **Second-opinion / record re-assembly changes management.** ✅ Van Such 2017 (J Eval Clin Pract, n=286 referred to Mayo): **21% got a distinctly different final diagnosis, 66% refined, 12% confirmed** → the widely-quoted "88% changed or refined." DOI 10.1111/jep.12747. **Caveat (state it):** selected referral population, single-center — evidence that *re-assembly changes referred/complex cases*, not that 88% of all diagnoses are wrong.
- **Infertility link.** ✅ ASRM: **30–50% of women with endometriosis are infertile; 25–50% of infertile women have endometriosis.** https://www.asrm.org/ · US IVF ~$15–30k/cycle all-in (Forbes Health). Don't assert a single "endo lowers IVF success by X%" — magnitude is study-dependent.

## 2. Why it's missed (the "assembly problem" thesis)

- **IBS overlap:** endo patients have **~3× the odds of an IBS label (OR 2.97)**, pooled IBS prevalence 23.4%. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9357916/
- **IC/bladder pain ("evil twins"):** Chung 2005 — of chronic-pelvic-pain women, **65% had BOTH** endometriosis and interstitial cystitis. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15791965/
- **Normalization of menstrual pain:** Ballard 2006 — delay occurs because *"both women and family doctors normalize symptoms."* https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17070183/
- Symptoms cross GI / urology / gyn / pain, so **no single clinician assembles the picture** — the structural gap Thaakat fills.

## 3. Imaging — why a "normal" scan isn't normal (grounds the re-read)

- **ESHRE 2022 dethroned laparoscopy** as the required gold standard; TVUS/MRI are first-line. NICE NG73: *"Do not exclude endometriosis if the examination and ultrasound are normal."* https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng73/chapter/recommendations
- **Superficial peritoneal disease is effectively invisible on TVUS and MRI** — the most prevalent, least-detectable phenotype. A normal scan does not exclude disease.
- **The realistic under-read** (what Maria's chart shows): junctional zone measured at **9 mm sits in the 8–12 mm indeterminate band** and gets dismissed; a spiculated T2-hypointense uterosacral nodule (deep infiltrating endometriosis) goes uncalled on a routine protocol. Adenomyosis JZ thresholds: <8 normal, ≥12 adenomyosis, 8–12 indeterminate (needs ratio >40% or JZ-diff >5 mm). Sources: Radiology Assistant; ESUR consensus 2025; Insights into Imaging JZ review.

## 5. Regulatory / safety framing (get this exactly right — an FDA-literate judge will check)

- **FDA "Clinical Decision Support Software" final guidance (Sept 28, 2022)** interprets the 21st Century Cures Act's four Non-Device CDS criteria. https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/software-medical-device-samd/clinical-decision-support-software-frequently-asked-questions-faqs
- **Criterion 1: software that acquires/processes/analyzes a medical image is, by definition, a device (SaMD)** — it cannot be "Non-Device CDS." So: **the record-assembly / text-and-data layer can qualify as non-device decision-support; the radiomics image re-read is investigational SaMD.** Say "decision support" for assembly; call the imaging re-read what it is. Do **not** claim the image tool is exempt.
- **Criterion 4 (independent review) = why we show the evidence.** Automation-bias data: Dratsch 2023 (*Radiology*) — a wrong AI suggestion dropped even 15+-yr radiologists from **82% → 45.5%** accuracy. Surfacing the assembled records + the feature driving the flag lets the clinician independently verify — both the FDA posture and the bias mitigation. https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/radiol.222176

## 6. The model (what's real; report AUC *with* n)

See [`radiomics/`](../radiomics/) and `lib/radiomics-model.json`.
- **Task:** endometriosis vs. no-pathology, real **GLENDA v1.5** laparoscopy frames (n=5,990; 500 pathology / 5,490 no-pathology; prevalence 8.35%).
- **Pipeline:** scikit-image GLCM + first-order texture (12 features) → RandomForest (300 trees, class-weight balanced) → 5-fold stratified CV, out-of-fold scoring (no leakage).
- **Result:** pooled OOF **ROC-AUC 0.966** (per-fold 0.967 ± 0.012), **average precision 0.864** at 8.35% prevalence. Top features: fo_entropy, glcm_homogeneity, fo_p10.
- **Honest caveats (say them first):** frame-level (not patient-level), single dataset, no external validation. Endo radiomics literature is **research-grade** — small, single-center, rarely externally validated (e.g. a published adnexal model dropped AUC 0.911→0.870 and F1 0.812→0.551 on external data). Production path: same pipeline on patient-level pelvic MRI (UT-EndoMRI, 133 real cases), external validation, prospective.
